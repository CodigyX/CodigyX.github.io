# Avatar angelical y evoluciones de Nex

Generados el 2026-10-12 mediante ImageGen integrado, no mediante API/CLI. Se conservaron los PNG originales; los recursos del sitio se redujeron y convirtieron a WebP con alfa, sin modificar la ilustración.

- `erick-angel-avatar.webp`: transformación personal basada en `perfil.png`, conservando la cara del usuario. 720 × 1080.
- `nex-galactic.webp`: primera forma existente de la mascota original, reutilizada sin cambios.
- `nex-guardian.webp`: segunda forma derivada de la referencia de Nex. 800 × 800.
- `nex-astral.webp`: tercera forma derivada de la referencia de Nex. 800 × 800.

No son modelos 3D ni renders de múltiples ángulos: la rotación del sitio anima las ilustraciones sobre una plataforma fija. Los diseños se solicitaron como personajes propios; esa intención no constituye un dictamen legal sobre derechos de terceros.

## Prompts finales

### erick-angel

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: transparent character avatar for a developer portfolio. Input image 1 is the identity reference, the real face of Erick; preserve his recognizable facial structure, skin tone, short black upward-styled hair, dark eyes and light beard. Primary request: portray this same adult man as a heroic angelic digital warrior inspired by the early-2000s cel-shaded monster-battle anime and console-game aesthetic. His face must be clearly visible, not hidden behind a visor and not changed to a blond anime character. Three-quarter upper-thigh portrait, calm confident expression, silver-white futuristic armor with violet inlays and restrained gold details, two large luminous feathered wings framing him, a slim geometric halo behind his head, elegant violet mantle, original asymmetrical star-shaped chest insignia, one gloved hand holding a vertical silver staff with a violet crystal. This is a personal original guardian interpretation, not the costume or exact design of any existing franchise character. Polished cel-shaded console character illustration, crisp silhouette, pearl light and violet rim light. Centered framing, all head, wings and staff visible with breathing room; face large enough to recognize on a 300px profile card. Truly transparent background, no platform, no text, no logos, no watermark.

### nex-guardian

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent evolution form of an original website companion. Input image 1 is the original mascot Nex, an orange fox digital creature with cream fur, long large ears, cyan eyes, silver armor, cyan heart-shaped energy core and violet crystals. Primary request: design its SECOND evolutionary form NEX GUARDIÁN, clearly the same species and character now taller and more heroic. Keep the orange and cream fur, cyan eyes, distinctive triangular ears, single bushy fox tail, violet ear crystals and heart-shaped cyan core. Increase to adolescent heroic fox proportions with larger paws, segmented silver-white shoulder and leg armor with restrained gold trim, violet panels, twin swept-back metallic energy fins on the back, rounded forearm gauntlets. Upright three-quarter pose facing slightly left, both feet touching an implied horizontal floor, arms relaxed ready, friendly determined face. Same polished cel-shaded early-2000s console/monster-battle anime illustration style and crisp readable silhouette as the reference. One creature alone full body centered in a square frame, ears, tail and feet fully visible, generous transparent margin, no platform, no background, no text, no logos, no watermark. Original evolution, do not reproduce any existing commercial character.

### nex-astral

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent ultimate evolution of an original website companion. Input image 1 is the original mascot Nex, an orange fox digital creature with cream fur, large triangular ears, cyan eyes, silver armor, cyan heart-shaped chest energy core and violet crystals. Primary request: design its THIRD evolutionary form NEX ASTRAL, clearly the same fox lineage transformed into a majestic celestial armored fox guardian, not a human angel. Keep the same expressive fox face, orange and cream fur, upright triangular ears, cyan eyes, violet ear crystals and heart-shaped cyan energy core. Tall heroic bipedal fox proportions, layered pearl-white and silver armor with violet inlays and restrained golden edge trim, four long swept-back segmented metallic energy wings radiating from a circular violet power unit on the back, a single large fox tail split into three flowing orange-gold ribbon-like fur tips, oversized gauntlets and armored digitigrade feet. Calm powerful three-quarter stance facing slightly left, arms relaxed, friendly vigilant face visible. Same polished cel-shaded early-2000s console monster-battle anime illustration style and crisp silhouette as the reference. Full body alone centered in a square frame, all ears, wings, tail and feet visible with generous transparent margin. No platform, no scenery, no background, no text, no logos, no watermark. Entirely original companion evolution, not a copy of an existing franchise character.
